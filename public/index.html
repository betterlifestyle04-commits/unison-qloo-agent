THE METHOD BEHIND THE MAGIC

Less compromise.
More common ground.

✧01 / DISCOVER

Understand each taste

We resolve favorite cultural references into Qloo entities and fetch recommendations for each person independently.

◇02 / BALANCE

Protect every voice

Our transparent group ranking considers the lowest relative fit and the group average. Missing evidence isn't mistaken for agreement.

↺03 / REPLAN

Make it yours

Compare how options fit everyone, veto a suggestion, and explore a new compromise with a visible decision trace.

UNISON scores are relative ranking heuristics, not probabilities of enjoyment or scientifically validated satisfaction measures. Qloo insights describe cultural affinities, not an individual's guaranteed behavior.
