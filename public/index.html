◈ Understand the data behind UNISON Qloo results · Fictional examples · Your saved plans
01 · Live Qloo signals

When selected, UNISON asks Qloo for cultural matches, then computes relative-fit rankings locally on the server. It does not verify opening hours, prices or bookability.

02 · Fictional demonstrations

Examples are synthetic and never presented as real films or venues. They let you explore the decision process without using Qloo.

03 · Your browser library

Only explicitly saved briefs and summaries are stored on your device. No account sync; backups are manual, readable JSON files containing personal details.

THE METHOD BEHIND THE MAGIC

Less compromise.
More common ground.

✧01 / DISCOVER

Understand each taste

We resolve favorite cultural references into Qloo entities and fetch recommendations for each person independently.

◇02 / BALANCE

Protect every voice

Our transparent group ranking considers the lowest relative fit and the group average. Missing evidence isn't mistaken for agreement.

↺03 / REPLAN

Make it yours

Compare how options fit everyone, veto a suggestion, and explore a new compromise with a visible decision trace.

UNISON scores are relative ranking heuristics, not probabilities of enjoyment or scientifically validated satisfaction measures. Qloo insights describe cultural affinities, not an individual's guaranteed behavior.
